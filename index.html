Which generated image do you prefer?

Thank you for helping. This takes about 4 minutes and is completely anonymous.

What you will do

You will see two rows of computer-generated images, one above the other. Both rows show the same kind of thing (both dogs, both palaces, and so on) but they were made by two different systems.

Pick the row you think looks better overall — more realistic, more convincing, fewer oddities. There is no right answer; go with your first impression.

  • Click a row, or its button, or press 1/2 (or ↑/↓).
  • Press Z to see both rows at full size if you want a closer look.
  • Make your browser window as wide as you can — the images are wide.

Privacy

No personal data is collected: no name, no email, no login. We store only your choices, how long each took, and your screen size, under a random ID.

Please use a wider window

The images in this study are very wide, and on a narrow screen they shrink so much that the comparison stops being meaningful.

Please open this link on a laptop or desktop, and widen the window (or rotate a tablet to landscape).
