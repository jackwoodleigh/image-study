Which set of images looks better?

A short visual comparison. About 4 minutes, completely anonymous.

What you'll do

You'll see two rows of AI-generated images from two different systems.

  1. Decide which images look the most realistic and diverse.
  2. Click that row, or press 1 / 2 (or ↑ / ↓).
  3. Press Z any time to inspect both rows at full size.

There's no right answer, and you can't go back — go with your first impression. A wide window helps, since the images are wide.

No personal data is collected — no name, no email, no login. We record only your choices, how long each took, and your window size, under a random ID.

A wider window is needed

The images in this study are very wide. On a narrow screen they shrink so much that the comparison stops being meaningful, so the data wouldn't be usable.

Please open this link on a laptop or desktop and widen the window, or rotate a tablet to landscape.
